How I Work

Principles

A few rules I try to keep consistent across my projects.

01

No Paywalls

Core functionality shouldn't be artificially locked behind subscriptions or premium tiers. If I build it, I want people to actually be able to use it.

02

Free Work

Public projects are made with accessibility in mind. My work is intended to remain free to access and use rather than turning everything into a paid service.

03

No Advertisements

I don't believe every project needs to be monetized through intrusive advertising. The work should stay focused on the people using it.
